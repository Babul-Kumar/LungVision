## 1. Executive Summary & Policy Statement

Reconstructing patient-specific 3D anatomical structures (specifically lung surfaces and thoracic envelopes) from paired frontal (posteroanterior / PA or anteroposterior / AP) and lateral chest radiographs requires paired data. In the preceding 3D reconstruction feasibility audit (`research/3d_reconstruction/FEASIBILITY_REPORT.md`), the governing determination was established as:

$$\mathbf{Governing\ Decision:}\quad \text{FEASIBLE AFTER ACQUIRING PAIRED DATA}$$

To conduct reproducible, scientifically valid research without violating institutional governance, patient privacy, or clinical ethics, this Access Plan defines the acquisition, credentialing, licensing, and compliance protocol for four target medical imaging cohorts:

1. **LIDC-IDRI / LUNA16** (Low-dose diagnostic thoracic CT volumes + lung nodule / lung segmentation masks).
2. **Indiana University Chest X-Ray (IN-CXR / Open-i)** (Paired frontal and lateral planar radiographs + radiology reports).
3. **MIMIC-CXR-JPG v2.1.0** (Paired frontal and lateral radiographs + longitudinal clinical records).
4. **MIDRC (Medical Imaging and Data Resource Center)** (Paired CXR and chest CT studies acquired within $\le 48\text{ hours}$ on identical patients).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Fundamental Ethical & Compliance Invariant:**
> Automated scrapers, credential bypasses, token spoofing, or attempts to circumvent institutional access gates are **strictly prohibited**. All credentialed datasets must be acquired via sanctioned, audited, and authenticated paths by authorized personnel holding valid human-subjects training certifications.

## 2. Dataset Specific Profiles & Acquisition Protocols

### 2.1. LIDC-IDRI / LUNA16 (Thoracic CT Volumes & Anatomical Masks)

* **Repository / Hosting Platform:** The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA), National Cancer Institute (NCI).
  * LIDC-IDRI Collection: `https://wiki.cancerimagingarchive.net/display/Public/LIDC-IDRI`
  * LUNA16 Challenge subset: Grand Challenge Platform (`https://luna16.grand-challenge.org/`)
* **Primary Modality:** Full thoracic helical CT scans (DICOM series; re-sampled `.mhd`/`.raw` in LUNA16).
* **Cohort Size:** 1,018 cases (LIDC-IDRI); 888 standardized thoracic CT scans $\le 2.5\text{ mm}$ slice thickness (LUNA16).
* **Licensing & Terms of Use:** Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License (CC-BY 3.0). TCIA Data Usage Policy applies.
* **Credentialing Requirements:**
  * Open Access for research and academic use.
  * No CITI certification strictly mandated by TCIA, but user registration on TCIA is required for batch API tokens.
* **Patient & Study Identifiers:**
  * Patient ID format: `LIDC-IDRI-0001` through `LIDC-IDRI-1012`.
  * Study Instance UID: `1.3.6.1.4.1.14519.5.2.1.6279.6001...`
  * Series Instance UID: Globally unique DICOM UID.
* **Modality Availability:**
  * Frontal Radiograph: Scanned planar scout/topogram images exist for some series in LIDC, but lack standard $1800\text{ mm}$ diagnostic PA geometry.
  * Lateral Radiograph: Not natively available as diagnostic films.
  * 3D CT Volume: Full 3D volumetric CT available for all 888 LUNA16 cases.
  * Lung Segmentation: Available via LUNA16 lung lobe masks (`lung_masks/`) generated by automated morphological algorithms and verified by radiologist review.
* **Sanctioned Acquisition Method:**
  * Primary CLI tool: `NBIA Data Retriever` (Command Line Interface v4.4+) utilizing `.tcia` manifest files.
  * REST API: TCIA NBIA REST API v2:
    ```bash
    # Query series metadata
    curl -X GET "https://services.cancerimagingarchive.net/nbia-api/services/v2/getSeries?Collection=LIDC-IDRI&Modality=CT" -H "Accept: application/json"
    ```
  * LUNA16 Academic Mirrors: Academic torrent / Zenodo repositories hosted by Grand Challenge under CC-BY 3.0.
* **Storage Footprint:** ~124 GB compressed; ~240 GB uncompressed for 888 volumes.

### 2.2. Indiana University Chest X-Ray Collection (IN-CXR / Open-i)

* **Repository / Hosting Platform:** U.S. National Library of Medicine (NLM), National Institutes of Health (NIH), Open-i Service (`https://openi.nlm.nih.gov/`).
* **Primary Modality:** Planar digital chest radiography (DICOM and PNG).
* **Cohort Size:** 7,470 chest radiographs from 3,955 radiology reports (3,851 unique patients).
* **Licensing & Terms of Use:** Public Domain / Open Access for scientific research. De-identified under HIPAA Safe Harbor.
* **Credentialing Requirements:**
  * Completely open access. No login or formal DUA required.
* **Patient & Study Identifiers:**
  * Report/Study ID: Integer identifier (e.g., `1`, `2`, ..., `3999`).
  * Image Filenames: `<study_id>_IM-<sub_id>-1001.png` or `CXR<study_id>_IM-<sub_id>_<view>.png`.
  * Modality Projection Tags: DICOM `ViewPosition` (`PA`, `AP`, `LATERAL`).
* **Modality Availability:**
  * Frontal Radiograph: 3,823 studies contain verified frontal projections (PA or AP).
  * Lateral Radiograph: 3,638 studies contain verified lateral projections.
  * **Paired Frontal + Lateral:** 3,612 studies have simultaneous, verified Frontal and Lateral projections acquired in the same examination session.
  * 3D CT Volume: **None**. IN-CXR is planar radiography only.
  * Segmentation: 2D lung masks available for a 247-image subset via Montgomery/Shenzhen/NIH benchmarks, but 3D ground truth is absent.
* **Sanctioned Acquisition Method:**
  * NLM Open-i API v1.0 or official NIH archive download link (`NLMCXR_png.tgz`).
  ```bash
  # Download official Open-i IN-CXR archive
  wget -c "https://openi.nlm.nih.gov/imgs/collections/NLMCXR_png.tgz"
  wget -c "https://openi.nlm.nih.gov/imgs/collections/NLMCXR_reports.tgz"
  ```
* **Storage Footprint:** ~86 GB (DICOM); ~14 GB (1024x1024 PNG).

### 2.3. MIMIC-CXR-JPG v2.1.0 (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center)

* **Repository / Hosting Platform:** PhysioNet, MIT Laboratory for Computational Physiology (`https://physionet.org/content/mimic-cxr-jpg/2.1.0/`).
* **Primary Modality:** High-resolution chest radiographs derived from hospital PACS.
* **Cohort Size:** 377,110 radiographs corresponding to 227,835 radiographic studies across 65,379 distinct patients.
* **Licensing & Terms of Use:** PhysioNet Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement (v1.5.0).
  * Re-identification of patients is strictly prohibited under Federal law and contractual DUA.
  * Redistribution of raw data or posting un-redacted images in public repositories is strictly prohibited.
* **Credentialing Requirements:**
  1. Complete CITI Program Training Course: *"Data or Specimens Only Research"* (Record ID required).
  2. Maintain an active, verified PhysioNet Researcher account.
  3. Sign and execute the MIMIC-CXR Data Use Agreement with institutional affiliation.
  4. Access is granted manually by the PhysioNet review board (typical turnaround: 2–5 business days).
* **Patient & Study Identifiers:**
  * Subject ID: `subject_id` (e.g., `10000032`).
  * Study ID: `study_id` (e.g., `50414267`).
  * DICOM SOP Instance UID: `dicom_id` (e.g., `0aab0749-35436d5c-...`).
  * View Position: `ViewPosition` column (`PA`, `AP`, `LATERAL`, `LL`).
* **Modality Availability:**
  * Frontal Radiograph: >240,000 images.
  * Lateral Radiograph: >85,000 images.
  * **Paired Frontal + Lateral:** ~74,200 studies contain both Frontal and Lateral projections acquired within the same radiographic encounter.
  * 3D CT Volume: Not included directly in MIMIC-CXR; however, cross-linkable to MIMIC-IV Clinical Database for patients who also received thoracic CT (chest CT studies cataloged in MIMIC-IV-ED / MIMIC-IV Radiology notes).
* **Sanctioned Acquisition Method:**
  * Authenticated PhysioNet CLI using user personal access token:
    ```bash
    # Secure authenticated sync (REQUIRES VALID CREDENTIAL COOKIE / TOKEN)
    wget --user <PHYSIONET_USER> --ask-password -r -N -c -np \
      "https://physionet.org/files/mimic-cxr-jpg/2.1.0/"
    ```
* **Storage Footprint:** ~560 GB (compressed JPEG format).

### 2.4. MIDRC (Medical Imaging and Data Resource Center)

* **Repository / Hosting Platform:** Gen3 Data Commons, University of Chicago / RSNA / ACR / AAPM (`https://data.midrc.org/`).
* **Primary Modality:** Multi-institutional COVID-19 and non-COVID thoracic imaging, including paired CXR and CT.
* **Cohort Size:** >150,000 imaging studies from >60,000 patients across dozens of clinical trial sites.
* **Licensing & Terms of Use:** MIDRC Data Use Agreement. Research use only.
* **Credentialing Requirements:**
  * Gen3 account creation via InCommon institutional credentials or Google OAuth.
  * Accept MIDRC Data Commons Data Use Agreement.
* **Patient & Study Identifiers:**
  * Submitters Case ID: `submitter_case_id`.
  * Study UID: Standard DICOM Study Instance UID.
* **Modality Availability:**
  * **Paired CXR and Thoracic CT:** ~1,200 unique patients have BOTH a diagnostic digital chest radiograph (PA/AP) AND a volumetric chest CT scan acquired within $\le 48\text{ hours}$ of each other.
  * This cohort constitutes the golden benchmark for real-world cross-modality reconstruction validation!
* **Sanctioned Acquisition Method:**
  * `gen3-client` official CLI using downloaded `credentials.json`:
    ```bash
    gen3-client configure --profile=midrc --cred=credentials.json --apiendpoint=https://data.midrc.org/
    gen3-client download-manifest --profile=midrc --manifest=midrc_paired_cxr_ct_manifest.json --download-path=./data/midrc
    ```
* **Storage Footprint:** ~1.2 TB for the 1,200 paired subject cohort.

## 4. Institutional Compliance, Security & Storage Governance

### 4.1. Patient Privacy & De-Identification Safeguards
1. **DICOM Header Scrubbing:** All DICOM files acquired must be audited to ensure compliance with the PS 3.15 Basic Application Level Confidentiality Profile:
   - Patient Name (`0010,0010`), Patient ID (`0010,0020`), Patient Birth Date (`0010,0030`), Institution Name (`0008,0080`), and Performing Physician (`0008,1050`) must be anonymized or pseudorandomized.
2. **Burned-In Pixel Text Auditing:** Scanned planar films frequently contain burned-in annotations in corner margins. An automated OCR/masking pass must inspect and crop non-anatomical margins before model ingestion.
3. **Re-Identification Prohibition:** No team member or algorithm shall attempt to link de-identified records with external public or private registries to ascertain patient identity.

### 4.2. Storage Architecture & Encryption Standards
* **On-Premise / Local Storage:** Data directory `data/3d_reconstruction/` must reside on an encrypted volume (BitLocker on Windows, LUKS on Linux, AES-256).
* **Access Control:** Read-only permissions for training processes; write permissions restricted to dataset administrators.
* **Version Control Hygiene:** Medical images (DICOM, MHD, RAW, NIfTI, JPG, PNG) must **NEVER** be committed into Git repositories. All data directories are protected by root `.gitignore` entries:
  ```gitignore
  data/
  *.mhd
  *.raw
  *.nii
  *.nii.gz
  *.dcm
  *.tcia
  ```
