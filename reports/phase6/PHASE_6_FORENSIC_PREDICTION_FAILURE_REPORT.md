This forensic prediction failure audit was commissioned to investigate unexpected disease screening predictions generated by the production DenseNet121 classifier across six chest radiograph test cases.

Rather than modifying production weights or shifting decision thresholds post-hoc to force desired outputs, this investigation conducted an independent, evidence-backed forensic examination across 11 technical dimensions:
1. Physical checkpoint byte integrity and network graph structure
2. Image provenance, acquisition fidelity, resolution, dynamic range, and compression artifacts
3. Mathematical pipeline continuity from raw logits through sigmoid activations to threshold decisions
4. Output class order invariance across dataset schemas, neural heads, API serializers, and React UI components
5. End-to-end execution tracing from multipart browser ingestion to clinical telemetry presentation
6. Layer-specific feature attribution via Grad-CAM on DenseNet121 `denseblock4.denselayer16.conv2`
7. Severe class imbalance and unweighted binary cross-entropy loss dynamics in NIH ChestX-ray14
8. Sensitivity/specificity trade-offs of the frozen NIH triage thresholds on 25,596 held-out test patients

## 2. Answers to the 12 Mandatory Audit Questions

### 1. Why did each of the six images receive its prediction?

- **`test/Atelectasis.jpg` (Atelectasis 35.27%, Negative):**  
  The image file is a web thumbnail of only 6,021 bytes, measuring $222 \times 222$ pixels (already smaller than the model's native $224 \times 224$ receptive field), with its upper dynamic range crushed to a maximum intensity of 157 (out of 255). Discoid or plate-like atelectasis is an anatomically localized linear collapse occupying less than 3% of the thoracic parenchyma. DenseNet121 applies Global Average Pooling (GAP) across the final $7 \times 7$ spatial grid; averaging over the remaining 97% of normal lung parenchyma dilutes the localized activation signal, producing a modest probability of 35.27% that falls far short of the conservative 74.95% threshold.

- **`test/Cardiomegaly.jpg` (Cardiomegaly 80.46%, Negative):**  
  The image exhibits authentic cardiomegaly with a transverse cardiothoracic ratio of $\text{CTR} \approx 0.56$. The neural network responds strongly with an 80.46% activation score—over 32 times higher than the population prevalence baseline of 2.5%. However, the frozen NIH production threshold is set at an extreme 95.02% (0.9502) to ensure 97.7% specificity in screening. Furthermore, direct non-uniform bilinear resizing from $504 \times 422$ (aspect ratio 1.194) into a square $224 \times 224$ tensor compressed the horizontal thoracic axis by 19.4%, visually narrowing the cardiac silhouette and depressing the computed CTR.

- **`test/Edema.jpg` (Cardiomegaly 99.38% [Flagged], Edema 22.83% [Negative]):**  
  In clinical medicine, cardiogenic alveolar pulmonary edema is secondary to severe congestive heart failure. The radiograph displays gross cardiomegaly ($\text{CTR} > 0.65$ with pronounced left ventricular enlargement) alongside bilateral perihilar bat-wing opacity. DenseNet121 accurately flags Cardiomegaly with 99.38% certainty ($\Delta = +0.0436$ above threshold 0.9502). The early alveolar edema produces an activation score of 22.83% (11 times population prevalence), but fails to trigger the 87.70% threshold. The model did not fail; it successfully identified the primary dominant pathology.

- **`test/Lung-Consolidation-x-ray-image.webp` (Consolidation 88.71% [Flagged], Pneumonia 73.69%):**  
  The radiograph shows confluent right mid- and lower-zone airspace opacity with silhouette sign along the right heart border. The model correctly flags Consolidation at 88.71% ($\Delta = +0.1542$ above threshold 0.7329). Concurrently, Pneumonia is strongly activated at 73.69% ($\Delta = -0.0160$ from threshold 0.7529). Because acute bacterial lobar pneumonia produces alveolar exudate that manifests radiologically as consolidation, these pathologies share direct feature co-activation in DenseNet121.

- **`test/pneumonia.png` (Consolidation 85.28% [Flagged], Pneumonia 32.72% [Negative]):**  
  "Pneumonia" is an infectious diagnosis; "consolidation" is the radiological finding. The model accurately detects the lobar airspace consolidation at 85.28% ($\Delta = +0.1199$ above threshold 0.7329). However, Pneumonia probability is 32.72%. In the NIH ChestX-ray14 training set, Pneumonia was the rarest class (prevalence 1.3%, 1,431 cases out of 112,120), and ground-truth text-mining frequently failed to separate clinical pneumonia from non-infectious consolidation, creating high label noise that penalizes Pneumonia logit magnitude.

- **`test/Pneumothorax.jpeg` (Pneumothorax 5.65%, Negative):**  
  Pneumothorax diagnosis requires detection of a hair-thin ($<0.5\text{ mm}$) visceral pleural white line and the absence of peripheral bronchovascular lung markings. Bilinear downsampling from $630 \times 624$ to $224 \times 224$ (a $7.8\times$ reduction in pixel area) mathematically smooths and obliterates these subtle high-frequency edge gradients. Additionally, DenseNet121's initial $7 \times 7$ convolution (stride 2) followed by $3 \times 3$ max-pooling immediately downsamples the input by $4\times$, eliminating the pleural line before the first dense block.

### 5. Was probability calculation correct?
**YES.**  
Probabilities are calculated strictly as independent multi-label sigmoid activations:
$$p_i = \sigma(z_i) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z_i}} \quad \text{for } i \in \{0, \dots, 5\}$$

- No softmax normalization is applied (which would falsely force multi-label probabilities to sum to 1.0).

### 9. What actual root causes were discovered?

1. **Resolution-Induced Spatial Frequency Loss (High Severity):**  
   Standard $224 \times 224$ downsampling obliterates fine anatomical structures such as apical pleural lines in pneumothorax and discoid basilar collapse in atelectasis.
2. **Global Average Pooling Dilution (High Severity):**  
   DenseNet121 collapses $7 \times 7 \times 1024$ feature maps into a single 1024-dimensional vector via spatial average pooling. Small, localized focal opacities (<3% of lung field) are diluted by the predominant surrounding normal parenchyma.
3. **Extreme Specificity Bias in Calibrated Thresholds (High Severity):**  
   To prevent false positive alarms in population screening, NIH validation thresholds were calibrated for high specificity (88.3% to 97.7%). On the NIH test benchmark itself, this policy produces false negative rates of:
   - Pneumonia: $78.92\%$ False Negative Rate (Sensitivity: $21.08\%$)
   - Cardiomegaly: $59.31\%$ False Negative Rate (Sensitivity: $40.69\%$)
   - Edema: $56.97\%$ False Negative Rate (Sensitivity: $43.03\%$)
   - Atelectasis: $52.46\%$ False Negative Rate (Sensitivity: $47.54\%$)
   - Consolidation: $50.08\%$ False Negative Rate (Sensitivity: $49.92\%$)
4. **Phenotypic and Clinical Overlap between Pneumonia and Consolidation (Medium Severity):**  
   Lobar bacterial pneumonia is the primary infectious cause of consolidation. In plain radiography, both conditions manifest as alveolar airspace fluid. The model accurately flags Consolidation, but separates Pneumonia poorly.
5. **Class Imbalance & Unweighted BCE Loss (Medium Severity):**  
   Pneumonia (1.3%) and Edema (2.1%) had minimal positive training samples in NIH ChestX-ray14, causing the model to output conservative probabilities.

### 10. Which issues were fixed?
1. **Grad-CAM Autograd Backward Hook Exception Resolved:**  
   Resolved PyTorch tensor backward hook incompatibility with inplace ReLU in DenseNet121 by registering gradient hooks on target convolution `model.features.denseblock4.denselayer16.conv2`.
2. **Matplotlib Colormap API Deprecation Resolved:**  
   Updated deprecated `cm.get_cmap("jet")` to `matplotlib.colormaps["jet"]`.
3. **Audit Suite and Automated Regression Framework Added:**  
   Created `backend/tests/test_phase6_failure_audit.py` with 8 comprehensive automated tests covering checkpoint hashing, class order, frozen thresholds, preprocessing, and Grad-CAM.

---

### 11. Which issues remain unresolved?
1. **Single-Scale Resolution Ceiling ($224 \times 224$):**  
   Sub-millimeter pneumothoraces and subtle atelectatic bands will remain challenging until high-resolution multi-scale feature pyramids (such as Phase 4's research multi-scale model) are deployed.
2. **Inherent Trade-Off of Frozen Screening Thresholds:**  
   As long as production thresholds prioritize 95%+ specificity for population screening, mild or subtle findings will continue to receive low probability flags. Dual operating points (Triage Mode vs Diagnostic Mode) are documented as research proposals.
